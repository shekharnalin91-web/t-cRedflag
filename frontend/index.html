THE INVISIBLE VULNERABILITY

"Nobody reads the fine print. That's exactly where the risk hides."

The average Terms of Service agreement contains over 4,000 words—requiring roughly 30 minutes of legal deciphering. Tech companies leverage this fatigue to slip in binding arbitration, perpetual content licenses, cross-site telemetry, and unilateral modification rights.

DETECT

Automatically identify potentially risky clauses across 14 cybersecurity and privacy categories, including device fingerprinting, third-party data sales, and class action waivers.

SCORE

Generate a deterministic 0–100 risk score and tier classification (SAFE, CAUTION, HIGH RISK) based on clause severity weights and exposure density rather than random numbers.

EXPLAIN

Translate confusing legalese into plain English. Understand exactly why a clause matters from an information security standpoint and receive clear, actionable defense recommendations.

CYBERSECURITY PERSPECTIVE

Consent can become a critical data-security blind spot.

When users blindly click "I Agree", they grant legal permission for background tracking, credential sharing, and biometric profiling. Attackers and unscrupulous vendors exploit this legal umbrella to bypass traditional endpoint safeguards without triggering intrusion alerts.
