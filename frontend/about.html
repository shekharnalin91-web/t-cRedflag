About T&C Red Flag Scanner

Empowering users to decode legal obfuscation, identify privacy-invasive terms, and preserve their digital sovereignty before consenting to software agreements.

THE PROBLEM

The Consent Fatigue Crisis

Over 95% of consumers accept legal terms and privacy policies without reading them. Companies intentionally draft documents spanning dozens of pages packed with dense legalese to discourage scrutiny.

Buried inside these agreements are aggressive terms: mandatory binding arbitration that eliminates court access, permission to record screen sessions, device fingerprinting, and rights to sell personal records to data brokers without compensation.

THE SOLUTION

Lightweight Automated De-Obfuscation

The T&C Red Flag Scanner is a specialized security utility designed to inspect agreements in milliseconds. It extracts clauses, scans for risk indicators across 14 cybersecurity categories, calculates an objective 0–100 risk score, and translates legal traps into clear, plain English.

Crucially, it is built with an offline-first local architecture, meaning your sensitive contracts and documents never need to be uploaded to a remote cloud server.

Our Core Three-Pillar Methodology

1. DETECT

Rule-based and pattern-driven NLP segmentation scans text sentence-by-sentence against a taxonomy of 14 privacy risk categories, identifying exact quotes containing clauses of concern.

2. SCORE

Computes a deterministic 0–100 score weighted by severity (High: 15–16 pts, Medium: 8–9 pts, Low: 4–5 pts) alongside exposure density, categorizing documents into SAFE, CAUTION, or HIGH RISK.

3. EXPLAIN

Translates convoluted legal rhetoric into concise English explanations, details why the term matters from a cybersecurity posture, and provides defensive actions for users.

Cybersecurity Relevance & Threat Modeling

In cybersecurity, human users are often considered the weakest link. However, this weakness is exacerbated when users are coerced through dark design patterns into legally signing away their perimeter defenses.

"Consent can become a data-security blind spot when users unknowingly authorize collection, tracking or third-party access."

By scanning terms prior to consent, users can identify third-party sub-processor supply chain vulnerabilities, prevent persistent hardware fingerprinting, and decline contracts with mandatory unilateral revision terms.

Future AI Architecture & Roadmap

The current version implements an efficient, zero-cost, local rule-based NLP pipeline. The architecture is engineered modularly so that advanced local LLMs (e.g. Llama 3, Gemma, Mistral) or cloud LLM APIs can be dropped in as classification plugins.
