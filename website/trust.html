Trust & Zero-Knowledge Privacy Center

Why you can trust T&C Red Flag Guard to protect your browsing without collecting your private data.

1. 100% Local-First Engine

Unlike traditional cloud-based security tools that send your web traffic and full page text to external servers, T&C Red Flag Guard contains its entire 18-category threat taxonomy and NLP regex parser directly inside the client browser extension package (rules_data.js and client_analyzer.js).

When you visit a website, the extension parses and scores the Terms of Service text entirely within your local browser memory.

2. Zero Browsing History & Zero Telemetry

We strictly believe that a privacy security tool must not violate the user's privacy in order to protect it:

  • No URL Tracking: We do not log or store the URLs of websites you visit.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to log in or create an account to use the browser extension or desktop app.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry trackers are present in the extension code.

3. No Automatic Consent Actions

T&C Red Flag Guard NEVER automatically clicks "I Agree", "Accept", or consents on behalf of the user. The software functions purely as an antivirus-style detection and warning system that highlights legal red flags, explains hidden risks in plain English, and empowers YOU to make informed legal decisions.

4. Open Threat Taxonomy & Transparency

All risk classification rules, weight formulas, sub-risk metric metrics (Privacy, Financial, Rights Waiver, Lock-In, Transparency, Security), and negation protections are completely open for review in the source code repository.
